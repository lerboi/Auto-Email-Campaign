Make someone up. Then go and meet them.

The character creator hands you a blank page and gets out of the way. Face, voice, temperament, the history they half-remember — all of it is yours to set, and October is a good month to build something that wasn't there before.

You are not filling in a form. You are deciding how somebody behaves at two in the morning, what they refuse to talk about, and how long it takes them to warm up. Get it right and they stop feeling built — they just answer, in a voice you picked, about the things you gave them.

Your October Build Kit

• You Write the Temperament: Warm, guarded, wicked, devoted — set how they behave before you have said a word to them.
• A Face and a Voice to Match: Build the look, then pick the voice that comes out of it — so a call sounds like the person you had in mind.
• Nobody Else Has Them: What you build exists on your account and nowhere else, and there is nothing held back about where you take it.
