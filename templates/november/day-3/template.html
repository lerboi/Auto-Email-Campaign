Most people use a tenth of the generator.

The image generator has three different modes, and the one you land on by default is the simplest of them. There is a fair amount sitting behind it that never gets opened.

Standard is the quick one. Advanced gives you the controls — composition, detail, how hard the model leans on your prompt. Natural Language means you describe the picture in a sentence and let it work the rest out. Pick wrong and you reroll; pick right and you get it first time.

What's Actually In There

• Three Modes, Different Jobs: Standard for speed, Advanced for control, Natural Language for describing it the way you would to a person.
• Five At Once: Batch up to five images in one go, then keep the one that worked instead of rerolling the same prompt over and over.
• The Prompt Library: Saved prompts and presets, so the thing that worked last week is one tap away rather than something you half-remember.
