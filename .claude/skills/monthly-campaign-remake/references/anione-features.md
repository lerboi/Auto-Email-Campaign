## Core Features

Anione is a premium AI anime character chat and image generation platform. Key selling points:

- **AI Chat with Anime Characters**: Deep conversations with official and custom characters
- **Custom Character Creation**: Build your own AI characters with full personality/appearance control
- **Custom Scenario Creation**: Design worlds and storylines for characters to inhabit
- **AI Image Generation**: Create anime-style images from text prompts
- **Voice Calls**: Real-time voice conversations with AI characters (premium)
- **Relationship Progression**: 25-level system from Stranger to Soulmate
- **Daily Wheel Spin**: Free daily rewards (tokens, discounts)
- **Unrestricted Environment**: No content filters on interactions

## Pricing & Packages

### Subscriptions
- **Silver Package**: $9.99/month — 200 AI images + unlimited text messages
- **Gold Package**: $29.99/month — 1000 AI images + unlimited text messages

### Token Packs (One-Time Purchase)
- Text tokens: ~$0.05/token base (volume discounts up to 40%)
- Image tokens: ~$0.10/token base (volume discounts available)

### Common Promotional Offers
- **Free token voucher** (Day 1): Typically 10 image tokens, redeemed via code at `/Profile?tab=redeem&code={CODE}`
- **Subscription discount** (Day 7): Typically 20% off, bringing Silver to ~$7.99/mo. Code used at checkout.
- **Token multiplier** (Day 9-10): Typically 1.5x bonus on all token pack purchases. Code at checkout.

## Token System

Three token types:
- **Text tokens**: Power AI chat messages
- **Image tokens**: Power AI image generation (1-2 tokens per image)
- **Voice minutes**: Power voice call sessions (1 min = 1 token, billed per minute)

Tokens come from purchases (persistent) or subscriptions (monthly reset).

## Character System

### Official Characters
Pre-built anime characters with detailed personas, backstories, and gallery images. Examples include popular anime characters.

### Custom Characters
Users can create their own characters with:
- **Custom appearance**: Full visual control via avatar upload
- **Custom personality**: Define voice, mannerisms, backstory
- **Custom scenarios**: Set the scene and context for interactions
- **Gallery items**: Up to 6 images/videos per character

**Email angle**: Emphasize creative freedom, total control over appearance and personality, and the unrestricted interaction environment.

## Scenario System

Users can build custom worlds/scenarios:
- Choose any setting (fantasy, sci-fi, slice-of-life, etc.)
- Define character roles and relationships
- Control mood and tone of interactions
- AI adapts and responds within the world you build

**Email angle**: Emphasize world-building, storytelling, being the "master of your universe", and the AI's ability to deeply understand and play within custom contexts.

## Image Generation

Three generation modes:
- **Standard**: Tag-based anime image generation (1 token/image)
- **Advanced**: Higher quality model (2 tokens/image)
- **Natural Language**: Describe what you want in plain English (2 tokens/image)

Features: batch generation (1-5 images), prompt library, presets, prompt enhancement via AI.

**Email angle**: "Visualize your characters", "bring your imagination to life", creation as a core experience.

## Voice Calls

Real-time voice conversations with AI characters:
- Characters speak with distinct voices and personalities
- Conversations feel natural with emotional expression
- Available as premium feature (costs voice tokens per minute)

**Email angle**: "Hear her voice", "take your conversations beyond text", premium intimacy.

## Daily Rewards (Wheel Spin)

- Free spin daily for all users
- Paid users get better wheel with higher rewards
- Rewards: tokens, discount codes, bonus spins
- Token type selection (choose image, text, or voice tokens)

**Email angle**: "Free daily rewards", "come back every day", engagement hook.
